01 / ABOUT

Built with
intention.

Hi, I'm Jiwon. I started building web applications with PHP and CodeIgniter, and now work with Java and Spring Boot. Changing stacks has taught me to focus on the problem first, then choose the tools that fit.

I care about what happens after a service goes live: how it performs when traffic grows, how reliably it runs, and how easily people can keep improving it. That curiosity also draws me to DevOps and practical AI tools.

Open source is an important part of how I learn. I enjoy exploring work shared in public, exchanging ideas with other developers, and sharing what I discover along the way.
